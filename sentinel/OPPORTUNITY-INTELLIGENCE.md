# Sentinel Opportunity Intelligence

Sentinel must look for upside as well as downside. Historical analogues are hypotheses, not proof of opportunity.

## Opportunity classes
- Emerging customer demand and underserved markets
- Technology adoption and productivity improvements
- Supply-chain substitutions and local service gaps
- Public grants, legitimate procurement, and time-limited competitions
- Recovery/rebuilding demand after disruptions
- Skills and training demand
- Consumer savings and risk-reduction opportunities

## Opportunity record
Capture ID, location, sector, observation date, original sources, historical analogues and counterexamples, concrete customer problem, target buyer, proposed product/service, evidence of demand, competitors, user-specific advantage, estimated startup cash and labor, time to first revenue, potential revenue range with assumptions, deadline, downside risk, legal/ethical restrictions, and next verifiable action.

## Ranking
Keep evidence confidence distinct from commercial attractiveness. Evaluate demand, competition, differentiation, cost, risk, margin, speed to cash, and ability to execute with available tools. Require a clear customer and a concrete validation step. No invented revenue or promises of profit. Rank low-cost, owner-controlled opportunities higher when otherwise comparable.

## Safety and evidence
Do not treat disasters as occasions for exploitative pricing, fear marketing, or misinformation. Include counterexamples and falsification conditions. Record rejected opportunities and outcome follow-up to measure actual conversion and profit, not just alerts. No automatic purchases, outreach, posts, or commitments.

## MVP acceptance criteria
- A dated opportunity card with at least one verifiable source and source-as-of timestamp
- Explain why it may matter now and which past patterns are comparable
- Show upside, downside, uncertainty, startup cost and first-customer experiment
- Explicitly label missing evidence and abstain when unsupported
- Weekly ranked digest plus exceptional time-sensitive alerts only after corroboration
- Separate historical similarity from validated forecast probability

This is a design specification; live feeds, opportunity scoring, notifications, and dashboard are not yet implemented.
