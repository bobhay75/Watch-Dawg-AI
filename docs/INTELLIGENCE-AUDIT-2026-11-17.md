| High | Second-resolution collection filenames overwrote raw evidence and normalized records on repeated runs. ## Remaining architecture and release constraints

1. **Legacy backend dependency risk remains outside the intelligence adapters.** A direct-pins-only backend `pip-audit --no-deps --disable-pip` returned 124 advisory rows across 15 packages (including duplicate advisory rows). Affected pins: aiohttp, black, certifi, dnspython, ecdsa, filelock, multidict, oauthlib, pillow, pyasn1, PyJWT, pymongo, python-jose, starlette and urllib3. Some have no listed fixed version; multiple remedies require major updates and coordinated FastAPI/Starlette changes. This was not an audited transitive environment. `emergentintegrations` could not be audited on PyPI; the vendor-hosted LiteLLM wheel could not be version-audited. Do not interpret the clean KEY-9 audits as clearance of this separate legacy backend. Its dependency/runtime migration needs working service/database integration tests before release. No new vendor, paid service or proprietary dependency was added by these fixes.
2. **Candidate screening is deliberately coarse.** Statewide bounding boxes and NOAA issuance times do not prove event identity, spatial footprint overlap, causal relationships or source independence. A NOAA flood alert and NASA fire event can still be screening candidates; a human must reject irrelevant pairs. National and Missouri NOAA feeds are overlapping views of the same publisher, not independent evidence. Coverage counts are not summed into corroborated incidents.
3. **Coverage is incomplete and historical.** EONET collection is limited to the first 100 open events and excludes non-point geometries from regional matching. The dashboard now states this cap. Missouri/Ozarks boxes are approximate; local area keywords indicate review priority, not county membership or impact. Saved snapshots have no automatic freshness guarantee; timestamps must be reviewed. Absence of candidates or alerts never implies safety or absence of events. Pagination, polygon-aware matching and a reviewed freshness policy are future work.
4. **Publication requires a separate human decision.** Every new collector/correlation output retains a false public-release flag; candidates remain `candidate_only_not_corroborated`, and corroboration remains unassessed. Source/license review is still required, including NASA catalog upstream material. No automated path promotes these files into the public asset allowlist. No private archaeological/site coordinates are read or introduced by the code changes. NASA public feed geometries stay in private raw evidence; normalized regional outputs retain only coarse flags and dates.
5. **Storage is local, not a complete evidence vault.** Digests are not signatures and do not prove the normalized fields were correctly derived or that publishers are independent. Legacy digest-less fixtures remain readable for compatibility. The latest derived dashboard/report can be regenerated and overwritten, while input evidence history is retained indefinitely. Backups, encryption at rest, disk quotas and explicit reviewer identity/approval records require an operational design. The scripts do not prune or upload local evidence.
6. **No release action is authorized by these test results.** Review this feature branch and resolve relevant remaining service risks before approving any deployment or merge. PR #27 and main were left unchanged.
